🚌 City Bus Driver 3D

You are the bus driver. Pick one of 5 routes, follow the glowing route on the mini-map, pick up your passengers, drive them through town — and when a passenger gets off, they will stand in front of the door. Switch to first-person, look right at them, and only then you get paid!

🚏 Pick up & drop off
Stop inside the glowing stop zone, press E to open the doors.
🎥 First person
Press V to sit in the driver seat — steering wheel + your hands. Click the bus to lock the mouse and look anywhere.
👀 Get paid by looking
After a passenger steps off and stands by the door, look at them for 1.5 seconds to collect the fare. No look = no money!
🗺️ Mini-map
The coloured line is your route, the yellow star is your next stop, the white arrow is your bus.
