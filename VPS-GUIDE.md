Your repository is configured with a real **Windows 10 Pro VM** inside GitHub Codespaces (`ghcr.io/dockur/windows` with KVM hardware acceleration).

## How to Connect & Use Windows 10
1. Open the launch link above, choose **4-core (16 GB RAM)** if available (2-core also works), and click **`Create codespace`**.
2. Once the Codespace finishes starting (~1 minute), go to the **`PORTS`** tab at the bottom.
3. Find port **`8006`** (*Windows 10 Desktop (Open in Browser)*) and click the **Globe icon (`Open in Browser`)** (it will also try to open automatically).
4. **First boot only (~5–8 minutes):** You will see Windows 10 downloading and installing **100% automatically** inside that browser tab — don't touch anything until the Windows 10 Desktop appears!
5. Once installed, you can use Windows 10 right inside your browser (mouse, keyboard, Edge browser, `.exe` apps). Future boots skip installation and load straight to your Windows 10 desktop in seconds.
